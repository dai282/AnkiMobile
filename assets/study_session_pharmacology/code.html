Mechanism of Action of SGLT-2 Inhibitors (e.g., Dapagliflozin, Empagliflozin)?

visibility ANSWER REVEALED

Inhibits Sodium-Glucose Cotransporter 2 in the renal proximal convoluted tubule, blocking glucose and sodium reabsorption, promoting glucosuria and natriuresis.

check_circle

Reduces HbA1c (~0.5–1.0%), promotes moderate body weight reduction (~2–3 kg), and lowers systolic BP via osmotic diuresis.

shield

Distinct cardioprotective and nephroprotective benefits in Heart Failure with reduced Ejection Fraction (HFrEF) and Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD).
